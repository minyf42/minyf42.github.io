I study how neurodivergent people process and understand information, and how systems designed around the majority misread those differences as deficits. I use what I learn to build accommodation tools, both software and hardware assistive technology, that fit each person before a mismatch turns into harm.

I came to this from the other end of the problem. As a pre-doctoral research assistant in Organizational Behavior at NYU Stern, I study workplace ADA charges, and found that discrimination against people with cognitive disabilities is among the hardest for complaint systems to recognize. A complaint comes after the harm: the job may already be lost, and the person must still prove a disability others cannot see. Understanding where mismatches begin is how we prevent them.
