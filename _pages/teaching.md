    <p>Teach IELTS reading and writing to two students in seventh and tenth grade. I design personalized lessons around each student’s age, English proficiency and learning preferences: vocabulary practice as games, material presented through images, video and text together, and a short hands-on task at the start of each topic so students build background knowledge before they read. In each session, I track where comprehension breaks down and rule out causes one at a time, from unfamiliar vocabulary to reading strategy, then teach each student the approach that fits how they read.</p>
